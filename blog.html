Immersive Technology!!!

Immersive technologies which comprises of Virtual technology, Augmented technology and mixed reality are among the fastest growing technologies today.Immersive technology creates or extends reality by immersing the user in a digital environment.

Virtual reality

Virtual reality uses VR headsets to allow an individual to experience an entirely different reality. The first VR device was invented by Morton Heilig in 1957 called Sensorama. Virtual reality is especially changing the gaming industry by offering gamers an environment where they can interact with in-game objects and move within the space.Players can explore historically accurate ancient ruins or futuristic cities with a level of realism that feels physically authentic. Some of the best VR products are Oculus quest, Nintendo Labo VR kit and SONY playstation VR. Many individuals may think VR is all about gaming but it can also be used in class. ClassVR uses VR tech to raise engagement and knowledge retention for students.

Medical technology

Medical technology covers tools, devices and procedures used to prevent, diagnose, monitor and treat patients. Medical technologies include pregnancy tests, MRI scans and ultrasound scans. A fascinating medical technology would be assistive exoskeletons which are used for rehabilitation of gait impairments, spinal cord injuries, stroke recovery, and for industrial applications to reduce fatigue and prevent musculoskeletal injuries. 3D printed organ models are revolutionising medical education by providing med students with detailed models from dental components to skulls which helps them understand surgical procedures in new ways.
